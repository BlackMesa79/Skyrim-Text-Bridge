Add real DLL load and thread-lifecycle regression checks. Retain native English typing, larger compact candidate text, emoji display, automatic focus detection and Modex Esc fixes.
新增真实 DLL 加载与线程生命周期回归检查。保留原生英文输入、更大更紧凑的候选文字、Emoji 显示、自动焦点检测和 Modex Esc 修复。
